## On the primal-dual dynamics of support vector machines (2018)

**Publication:** IEEE Mathematical Theory of Networks and Systems (MTNS), Hong Kong 2018

This paper analyzes the dynamical behavior of support vector machine training algorithms from a primal-dual optimization perspective.
