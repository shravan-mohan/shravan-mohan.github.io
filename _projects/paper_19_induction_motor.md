## An improved PWM for a dual two-level inverter fed open-end winding induction motor drive (2010)

**Publication:** IEEE International Conference on Electrical Machines (ICEM), Rome 2010

This research presents improved pulse-width modulation techniques for dual two-level inverter systems driving open-end winding induction motors.
