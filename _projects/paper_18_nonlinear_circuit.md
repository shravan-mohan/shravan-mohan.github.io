## Some stability analysis of a non-linear time-delayed feedback circuit (2012)

**Publication:** IEEE Chinese Control and Decision Conference (CCDC), Taiyuan 2012

This paper analyzes the stability of non-linear feedback circuits with time delays using Lyapunov theory and bifurcation analysis.
